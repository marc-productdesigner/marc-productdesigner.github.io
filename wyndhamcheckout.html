Before the Redesign

The existing checkout had several usability problems: unclear information hierarchy, inconsistent UI patterns, and a visual design that hadn't kept up with modern expectations for booking flows.

Research

Competitive Analysis

I analyzed checkout flows across 10 major booking and travel platforms to benchmark Club Wyndham against industry leaders and identify patterns worth adopting.

Agoda
Airbnb
Apple
Booking.com
Google Shopping
Groupon
Hotels.com
Hotwire
Orbitz
Southwest

Key Discoveries

  • Rate of checkout abandonment in mobile is high compared to desktop.
  • The average number of pages in a checkout flow is 4.
  • The 2 most common reasons for checkout abandonment are unexpected costs and long, complicated checkout process.

Solution

The redesigned checkout brought a clean, modern visual language, clearer information hierarchy, and a streamlined multi-step flow that reduced cognitive load and guided users confidently through to booking completion.
