Spend less time deciding what to watch.

People need a way to do a quicker and more efficient selection process when picking a movie or tv show to watch because they value time and quality programming.

Problem

People need a way to do a quicker and more efficient selection process when picking a movie or tv show to watch because they value time and quality programming.

Solution

Develop a mobile app that allows users to view what movies and TV shows their friends are watching to help users take less time to decide what to watch.
