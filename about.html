Hello! I'm Marc Laurente.

Senior product designer at Rentvine. Previously Ziff Davis Shopping, Cars.com, Wyndham Destinations, Benjamin Moore & various creative agencies. Also worked at Apple Inc. as a creative trainer.

My passion for design, technology, and empathy for users started when I worked at Apple Inc. I taught my fellow colleagues how to use Apple's latest hardware and software, but teaching a customer how to use a computer for the first time was more rewarding.

I am also an avid photographer. I used to run my own photography business, working with a wide variety of clients — social media influencers, couples in love, and adorable children. I dabbled in some video projects as well.
